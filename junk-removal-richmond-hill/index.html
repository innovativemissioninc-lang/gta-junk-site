Where do large appliances and bulky items go in York Region?

York Region operates Community Environmental Centres and waste depots that accept bulky items and large appliances, including the Richmond Hill Community Environmental Centre (1124 Elgin Mills Road East), the Vaughan Community Environmental Centre (130 McCleary Court), and the Markham Household Hazardous Waste Depot (430 Rodick Road). Disposal fees apply to items dropped off as garbage. Verified against York Region's Waste Depots pages, July 2026.

What if the item is still in good condition?

York Region encourages donating items in good condition to charity rather than disposing of them. That matches how we work — usable furniture, tools and household goods are staged for local donation partners before anything is routed to disposal. Verified against York Region's waste disposal guidance, July 2026.

Do I need to check what's accepted before I go?

Yes — accepted materials, hours and fees vary by depot, and York Region's Bindicator tool is the authoritative source for a specific item. We'd rather you check than drive across the region with a load they won't take. Verified against York Region's Bindicator, July 2026.
