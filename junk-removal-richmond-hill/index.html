Where the most thorough finish earns its name

Richmond Hill books our top finish more than anywhere else we serve. Bayview Hill's substantial garages, Mill Pond's character homes, Oak Ridges properties backing onto the moraine — these aren't spaces anyone wants merely emptied. They want them returned to standard: floors degreased, corners genuinely dust-free, and whatever stays organised and staged.

The collector's garage

Richmond Hill has more than its share of serious garages, and serious garages accumulate seriously: seasonal tire stacks, project-car parts, and equipment for properties with real acreage. Our crews clear around what stays — the vehicles, the tools that matter — with full protection on floors and finishes, then detail the space so what remains finally has room to breathe.

Moving up, moving on

Families here move for good reasons: larger homes, downsizing after the children launch, estates passing along. Whole-property clearings handle the transition — sorted with the family, donations staged for York Region charities, personal items protected, and the home handed over showable for its next chapter.

What a Richmond Hill Cleanout Includes

Four commitments that hold on every job, regardless of size or finish level.

Fixed Price

Quoted before anything moves
