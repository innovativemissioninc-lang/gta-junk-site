Scarborough homes work hard

From the Bluffs to Agincourt, Scarborough's bungalows and back-splits have hosted decades of real family life — and their full-height basements, double garages, and generous lots have stored every chapter of it. When it's finally time to clear — a downsizing, an estate, a basement reclaiming, a move — the volume can feel unclimbable from the kitchen table. It isn't. It's one visit for us.

The basement reclamation

Our signature Scarborough job is the full basement: furniture from three apartments ago, holiday decorations in strata layers, the freezer that stopped working during the lockdowns. Crews work methodically — sort, wipe, carry — with runners on the stairs and every personal item (photo albums live in basements) set aside in a clean bin before anything is loaded. Then the finish: swept, cobwebs down, ready to be usable square footage again.

Estates along the Bluffs

Guildwood and Cliffcrest's long-held homes deserve unhurried, respectful estate cleanouts — which is exactly Rocky's pace. Donations are staged for east-GTA charities and community organizations first; usable goods from Scarborough homes have a long second life ahead of them.

Landlords and multiplex turnovers

Scarborough's rental stock keeps property managers busy. Unit and whole-multiplex turnovers get cleared, swept, and showable with COIs supplied and one itemized invoice.
