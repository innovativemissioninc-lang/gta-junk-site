Etobicoke: where the deep garage lives

Etobicoke's post-war neighbourhoods — the Kingsway, Sunnylea, Humber Valley, Alderwood — were built when garages were meant to hold a car and a workshop. Seventy years later they hold everything else: inherited furniture, three generations of tools, and the lawn equipment arms race of a mature-tree neighbourhood. These deep single and double garages are our favourite canvas, because the transformation is dramatic — packed to the rafters in the morning, bare degreased concrete by dinner.

Bungalow basements and estate homes

The same streets hold Etobicoke's other specialty: the long-held family home. Estate and downsizing cleanouts in the Kingsway and Princess-Rosethorn get our discreet crews — valuables and photographs set aside before anything is loaded, donations receipted where possible, and a finish that lets the realtor book the photographer with confidence. See our estate cleanout service for how we handle these with care.

Humber Bay's condo shoreline

Along Lake Shore and Park Lawn, Humber Bay's towers turn over constantly. We handle unit cleanouts with the elevator bookings, dock windows, and COIs building management expects — cleared and showable inside the booked window.

Access from our crews' routes along the Queensway and 427 keeps Etobicoke response times among our fastest — same-day slots are often available.
