A city with no room for clutter — literally

Toronto living squeezes storage from every side. Condos downtown come with lockers the size of a closet. Victorian semis in Riverdale and the Annex hide steep, narrow basements that swallow boxes and never give them back. The Beaches and Leslieville have laneway garages that haven't seen a car since the Leafs' last Cup run. When space is this expensive per square foot, a cleanout isn't housekeeping — it's reclaiming real estate.

How we work downtown

Condo cleanouts need choreography: elevator bookings, loading-dock windows, property-management sign-off. Our crews handle that routine weekly — we bring the COI your building manager asks for, pad the elevator like it's ours, and clear a unit inside the booked window. For the narrow Victorians, careful matters more than fast: floor runners on the original hardwood, corners guarded on the plaster, every item wiped before it travels up the stairs.

From Rosedale to the laneways

In Rosedale, Lawrence Park, and Forest Hill, estate and downsizing cleanouts get our most discreet crews — quiet operations, valuables and documents set aside room by room, and a finish worthy of the listing. In the east end, our garage cleanout crews turn laneway garages back into parking, storage, or that studio you've been planning.

Donation-first is a Toronto value and ours too: usable goods are staged for local donation partners before anything heads down the DVP to a transfer station.

Do you do condo cleanouts in downtown Toronto?

Yes — weekly. We coordinate elevator bookings and loading-dock windows with your property manager, bring the certificate of insurance buildings require, and clear the unit inside the booked window.

Can you clear a laneway garage with tight access?

That's classic east-end Toronto work. Tight laneways, narrow doors, and no driveway are all workable — we assess access during the free on-site valuation and plan the haul route before anything moves.

How much does junk removal cost in Toronto?
