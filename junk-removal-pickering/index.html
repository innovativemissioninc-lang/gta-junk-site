Pickering Disposal & Pricing Questions

How does large item pickup work in Pickering?

Durham Region collects furniture and large items on your regular biweekly garbage collection day with no appointment needed, and Pickering residents can set large and bulky items out on regular collection day. A separate truck handles large items, so they are not picked up at the same moment as your bags. Verified against Durham Region's Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

How many items per collection?

The Region collects up to two items per pickup. For anything beyond a couple of pieces — a full estate, a basement, a garage — that cadence does not work, which is when a single-visit clearing makes more sense. Verified against Durham Region's Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

What needs an appointment?

Metal goods, electronics and porcelain require appointments, unlike general furniture. Durham can be reached at 311 within Regional limits, or 905-668-7711. Verified against Durham Region's Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.
