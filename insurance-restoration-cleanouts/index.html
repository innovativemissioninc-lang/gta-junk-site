The debris is not the problem. The documentation is.

On a restoration job the clearing itself is straightforward — it is volume and weight and a crew who will work in a wet basement. What actually causes trouble is the paperwork. An adjuster who cannot see what left the building, how much it weighed and where it went will question the line, and the contractor absorbs the delay.

We work the way a restoration file needs us to. Dated photographs before the haul and after it, scale tickets for anything priced by tonnage, a record of where material was routed, and one itemised invoice rather than a running tally. It arrives as a package you can attach to the claim.

We are registered with Ontario’s Ministry of the Environment as a non-hazardous waste transportation system, registration R-004-5477911257, and we carry $2 million in commercial general liability. On a restoration site that is not a marketing line — it is the reason a GC can put us on the schedule without a conversation with their own insurer.

Good Questions

Insurance and Restoration Debris Removal — Questions We Get Asked

How much does restoration debris removal cost?

Insurance and restoration debris removal starts at $1,800. Beyond that it is driven by volume and tonnage, since fire and flood debris is heavy and most of the cost is at the scale. Heavy material is priced by the tonne with scale tickets provided, so the invoice reconciles against the weight rather than an estimate. A 30% deposit confirms the booking, waived for established Net-30 trade accounts.

What documentation do you provide for the insurance file?

Dated photographs before and after the haul, scale tickets for tonnage-priced material, a record of where each waste stream was routed, and a single itemised invoice. It is supplied as one package rather than piecemeal, because adjusters question what arrives in fragments.

Do you handle asbestos or contaminated material?

No. Our MECP registration covers non-hazardous waste transportation only. Suspected asbestos-containing material, PCBs, biomedical waste, radioactive material, fuels and unidentified chemical containers are outside it. On older demolition work this comes up regularly — we flag it, stop, and refer you to a correctly licensed abatement contractor. Loading it would expose your file as well as ours.

Can you respond on an emergency basis?

Yes. Restoration and insurance work has priority dispatch including weekends, and we schedule around drying equipment and trade sequencing rather than around our own convenience. Call (647) 204-0632 and you will reach us directly, not a call centre.

Do you offer trade accounts and Net-30 terms?

Yes. Restoration companies, general contractors and property managers can run a Net-30 account with certificates of insurance held on file and a single monthly statement covering multiple sites. Ask at the first job and we will set it up.
