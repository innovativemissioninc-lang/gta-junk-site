Estate-scale, by default

King Township builds on a different scale. Multi-acre lots, four-bay garages, coach houses, barns and workshops mean a single property here can hold more than an entire street elsewhere in the GTA. Companies that arrive with a pickup truck discover that halfway through the morning.

Long driveways change the job

A two-hundred-metre gravel drive means every item is carried further, and it means protecting a surface that shows every tyre mark. We plan staging positions before we start and work outward rather than dragging loads the full distance repeatedly.

Discretion in a small community

King City, Nobleton and Schomberg are places where people know each other and notice a truck sitting outside a neighbour's house for a day. Estate and downsizing work here gets our quietest crews, unmarked staging where you'd prefer it, and no production made of the job.

Outbuildings, workshops and machinery

Barn and workshop clearings are the most commonly mishandled work in this area. Metal is separated for recycling rather than landfilled, serviceable equipment is set aside for donation or resale, and anything containing fuel or chemicals is routed properly. That sorting is the difference between a cleanout and a dump run.

Pre-listing work

King Township properties photograph on their space. A cleared, detailed outbuilding reads as usable square footage to a buyer; a full one reads as someone else's problem.

King City Disposal & Pricing Questions

Where do bulky items and appliances go in York Region?

York Region runs Community Environmental Centres that take bulky items and large appliances — the Vaughan CEC at 130 McCleary Court is the closest to Woodbridge. Disposal fees apply on items dropped as garbage. Verified against York Region's Waste Depots pages, July 2026.

What if it's still in good condition?

York Region encourages donating usable items to charity rather than disposing of them, which matches how we work — anything serviceable is staged for local donation partners before disposal is even considered. Verified against York Region's waste guidance, July 2026.
