Older Ajax, and what is stored in it

Ajax grew in waves, and the housing stock shows it. The older streets around Pickering Village and Harwood south of the 401 hold properties that have been in the same family for thirty or forty years, with full basements, detached garages and the kind of accumulated storage that only becomes visible when someone has to clear it. These are the estate and downsizing jobs we are usually called for in Ajax, and they are rarely one truckload.

Downsizing on the lakeshore

The Riverside and lakeshore pockets have an older demographic and a steady flow of downsizing moves — a large family home traded for a condominium or a retirement residence, with forty years of contents that will not fit. We handle the clearing side of that: what goes to the new place is boxed and left, what the family wants is set aside, usable goods are routed to Durham charities, and the house is handed back showable for the agent.

New-build Ajax and renovation debris

North Ajax and Duffin Heights are newer, and the work there looks different — basement finishing debris, deck teardowns, builder leave-behinds, and the contents of a garage that has been the staging area for two years of projects. Renovation debris is priced by volume and weight with scale tickets provided, so the invoice reconciles against the scale rather than an estimate.

Durham collection is slower than people expect

Durham Region does collect furniture and large items, but at a pace that suits a couple of pieces rather than a property. For a full estate, a basement or a garage, waiting out a two-item-per-cycle schedule is not realistic, which is when a single-visit clearing makes more sense.

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Ajax?

Estate cleanouts start at $2,000 for a standard clear and $3,200 for our Signature finish. Pre-listing cleanouts start at $1,500, hoarding projects at $4,000, and commercial work at $2,500. Your exact price depends on volume, weight, access and the finish level you choose, and it is fixed in writing at a free on-site valuation before a single item moves. A 30% deposit confirms the booking and comes off the final invoice.

How does large item pickup work in Ajax?

Durham Region collects furniture and large items on your regular biweekly garbage collection day with no appointment needed, and a separate truck handles them, so they are not picked up at the same moment as your bags. The Region collects up to two items per pickup. Verified against Durham Region’s Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

What needs an appointment in Durham?

Metal goods, electronics and porcelain require appointments, unlike general furniture. Durham can be reached at 311 within Regional limits, or 905-668-7711. Verified against Durham Region’s Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

Can you clear an Ajax property before it goes on the market?

Yes, and it is one of our most common Durham jobs. We clear the full property, set aside photographs, documents and anything personal for the family to review, route usable furniture to donation, and hand the house back swept and showable for listing photographs. Tell us your MLS date at the valuation and we will book backwards from it.

Do you offer same-day service in Ajax?

Same-day and next-day slots are available in Ajax subject to crew routing. Restoration companies and property managers get priority emergency response. Call (647) 204-0632 for today’s availability.
