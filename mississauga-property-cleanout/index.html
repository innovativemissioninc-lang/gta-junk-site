Who does whole-property cleanouts in Mississauga?

We do. Mississauga property cleanouts run the full range — a Malton bungalow being cleared for sale, a Streetsville family home after a downsizing decision, a Port Credit condo turnover, or a rental unit that emptied badly. The distinction from junk removal is scope: the whole property is cleared as one project, sorted donation-first, and handed back to whatever finish level you choose rather than simply emptied.

Malton is minutes from our base

Our base sits in northwest Etobicoke, which puts Malton closer to us than most of Toronto is. That proximity is not a marketing line — it is the difference between "we can fit you in later this week" and "we can be there this afternoon." Companies quoting Malton from a downtown or west-Mississauga yard are treating it as an outlying run; we are not.

The Peel appliance rule that catches people out

This is the single most useful thing to know about clearing a property in Mississauga: Peel Region no longer collects large appliances from the curb. An old fridge, freezer, washer or dryer will sit exactly where it is unless someone drives it to a Community Recycling Centre. On a property cleanout that matters, because appliances are usually the heaviest and most awkward items in the house. We take them as part of the load. Verified against Peel Region — Bulky Items, July 2026.

Condo and rental turnovers

Condo turnovers around Port Credit and along the Hurontario corridor have their own constraints: elevator bookings, loading-dock windows, and a concierge who needs to know who is in the building and when. We schedule around the building's rules rather than turning up and hoping, which is generally the difference between a turnover that finishes in a day and one that gets stopped at the door.

Estate clearing and downsizing

Estate work is handled by crews who understand what they are walking into. Photographs, documents and anything that looks personal are set aside for the family to review before anything is loaded. Usable furniture is routed to donation. For downsizing, the household sets the pace and nothing leaves without agreement — the donate pile stays visible for exactly that reason.

Renovation and pre-sale clearing

Pre-sale purges are about how the property photographs and shows, so the finish level matters more than the volume. Renovation clearing is the opposite problem — heavy, dense material that is priced by weight, with weigh tickets supplied so you can see what was actually hauled. We will tell you which of the two you actually need when we walk it.

We do — GTA Junk and Property Cleanout clears whole properties across Mississauga, including Malton, Streetsville, Erin Mills and Port Credit. Does Peel Region collect large appliances from the curb?

No. Peel Region no longer collects large appliances such as fridges, freezers, washers and dryers at the curb — they must be taken to a Community Recycling Centre. This is the rule that most often leaves an old appliance sitting in a Mississauga or Brampton garage indefinitely. We haul them as part of the cleanout. Verified against Peel Region — Bulky Items, July 2026.

Can you work around condo elevator and loading-dock bookings?
