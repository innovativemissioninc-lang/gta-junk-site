Frequently Asked Questions

What do cleanouts start at in the GTA?

We price by the job, not by the hour. Estate cleanouts start at $1,200 and our signature estate finish at $2,500. Pre-listing and realtor-ready cleanouts start at $1,500, insurance and restoration debris removal at $1,800, commercial cleanouts at $2,000, and hoarding projects at $3,500. Jobs over $2,000 take a 30% deposit to hold the crew and the disposal slot. These are honest starting points, not teaser rates — your exact price depends on volume, weight, access, and how far you want the finish taken, and it is fixed at a free on-site valuation before a single item moves. We don't quote blind over the phone because we'd have to pad the number to protect ourselves, and you'd pay for our guesswork.

How much does junk removal cost in Toronto?

Every space is different, so we don't guess over the phone. We visit, walk the space with you, and fix a firm price on the spot — before a single item moves. You choose how far the finish is taken — cleared and swept, cleaned and reset, or fully detailed — and the price is locked before we start. Jobs over $2,000 take a 30% deposit. Request a free on-site valuation to get your number.

Do you do same-day property cleanouts in the GTA?

Yes — same-day and next-day slots are available across Toronto and the GTA, subject to crew availability. Commercial clients like property managers and restoration companies get priority emergency response. Call us or request a valuation online and we'll confirm a 2-hour arrival window.

Do you handle estate and insurance/restoration cleanouts?

Both. Estate cleanouts are handled by discreet crews who set aside photos, documents, and valuables before anything is loaded, with donation-first sorting. For restoration and insurance work, we clear flood and fire debris in a single continuous haul and provide documentation — including weigh tickets — ready for the insurance file.

What items can't you take?

We can't take hazardous materials: paint, solvents, chemicals, propane tanks, asbestos-containing material, and biohazards. Nearly everything else — furniture, appliances, electronics, renovation debris, hot tubs, and full-property contents — is fair game. If you're unsure about an item, ask during your valuation and we'll point you to the right disposal channel.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are registered with Ontario’s Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks as a waste transportation system — registration number R-004-5477911257 — and we carry $2 million in commercial general liability insurance. Certificates of insurance are issued on request for property managers, estate lawyers and commercial accounts. Your property is protected before we lift — runners down, corners guarded, doorways padded.

Who can remove a hot tub in Etobicoke this week?

We can — hot tub removal is one of our regular jobs, and Etobicoke is our home base, so same-week (often same-day) slots are realistic. We disconnect, dismantle, and haul the whole tub, then sweep the pad. Call (647) 204-0632 or book a free on-site valuation and we'll confirm an arrival window.

Can you clean out my garage this week?

Yes. Same-day and next-day garage cleanout slots are available across Toronto and the GTA, subject to crew availability. A typical single-garage cleanout is one visit — packed to the rafters back to a swept or bare-concrete floor. Request a valuation and we'll lock a two-hour arrival window.

How do I get rid of an old fridge or freezer in Mississauga or Brampton?

You have us take it, because Peel Region no longer collects large appliances from the curb — an old fridge or freezer will sit in your garage indefinitely otherwise. We haul it as part of the load and route it to proper appliance recycling. (Rules verified against Peel Region, July 2026; confirm before relying on them.)

Do you take away just one item, like a single couch or mattress?

Yes — single-item pickups are welcome, whether it's a couch, mattress, fridge, treadmill, or piano. Small jobs have a minimum charge, and we'll give you the firm number before we load. Usable furniture is donated and mattresses are recycled wherever possible rather than landfilled.

Are you licensed and insured, and will you protect my floors?

Yes to all three. We're a licensed and insured Ontario company, we lay floor runners and protect door frames on every job, and our crews carry items rather than dragging them. We leave your walls and floors exactly as we found them — the space just gets emptier.
