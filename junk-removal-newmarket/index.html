Heritage homes off Main Street

The streets around historic Main Street South hold genuine heritage properties, and clearing one is a different job from clearing a subdivision house. Narrow staircases, original trim that cannot be scuffed, small doorways, and no rear access. We protect the route before anything moves — runners, stair protection, padded frames — because on a heritage property the damage costs more than the haul.

A town that downsizes

Newmarket has a large settled retirement population and a steady flow of moves into the condominium and retirement stock along Davis Drive and Yonge. That produces our most common Newmarket job: a family home of thirty or forty years cleared so it can be listed, with the owner moving to somewhere a fraction of the size. We box what is going with them, set aside what the family wants, and donate the rest locally.

Estate work for out-of-town executors

A great many Newmarket estates are administered by children who live in Toronto, Ottawa or out of province. We work unattended through a realtor or lockbox, send a photographed walkthrough before anything moves and a second set at completion, and confirm everything by text. Several of our York Region estate clients have never been inside the property.

Glenway, Stonehaven and the garage problem

The larger subdivisions produce the familiar pattern: a double garage that has not held a car since 2011, a basement storage room that became a storage floor, and a shed. Our garage finish takes it back to bare, degreased concrete in a single visit rather than simply emptying it.

Newmarket Cleanout & Disposal Questions

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Newmarket?

Estate cleanouts start at $2,000 for a standard clear and $3,200 for our Signature finish. Pre-listing cleanouts start at $1,500, hoarding projects at $4,000, and commercial work at $2,500. Fixed in writing at a free on-site valuation before a single item moves, with a 30% deposit confirms every booking that comes off the final invoice.

Where do bulky items and large appliances go in York Region?

York Region operates Community Environmental Centres and waste depots that accept bulky items and large appliances, including the Georgina Transfer Station, the East Gwillimbury depot and the Community Environmental Centres at McCleary Court in Vaughan and Elgin Mills Road East in Richmond Hill. Disposal fees apply to items dropped off as garbage, and fee schedules change — confirm the current rate on York Region’s waste depot pages before you load the car.

Can you clear a heritage property without damaging it?

That is the main thing we are being paid for on those jobs. Floor runners, stair protection and padded door frames go in before the first item moves, and crews carry rather than drag. Original trim, plaster and staircases in the Main Street heritage pocket are not replaceable, and we treat the route as part of the job rather than an afterthought.

Can you handle the cleanout if I live outside Ontario?

Yes, and it is common in Newmarket. We arrange access through a realtor, lawyer or lockbox, send a photographed walkthrough before anything moves, confirm by text what is being set aside, and send a second photographed set at completion. Invoicing and payment are handled remotely.

Same-day and next-day slots are available subject to crew routing. Call (647) 204-0632 for today’s availability.
