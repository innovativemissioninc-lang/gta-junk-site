Junk removal that respects the house it's leaving

Most haulers treat junk removal as a race between the truck and the curb. We treat it as work happening inside your home. Before anything lifts, our crew lays floor runners, pads the doorways, and guards the corners. Then every item — even the ratty old couch — gets a quick wipe-down before it travels through your hallway. Rocky's rule: dust off, then load.

What we take

  • Furniture — couches, mattresses, wardrobes, sectionals that "don't fit through the door" (they will)
  • Appliances — fridges, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves
  • Electronics — TVs, monitors, e-waste, responsibly routed to certified recyclers
  • Renovation debris — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures
  • Hot tubs, sheds, and workbenches — our crews specialize in heavy, awkward extractions
  • Yard waste, tires, scrap metal, and the "miscellaneous" pile you've been avoiding

What we can't take: hazardous materials — paint, solvents, chemicals, propane tanks, asbestos-containing material, and biohazards. Ask during your valuation and we'll point you to the right disposal channel.

Donation first, landfill last

Every load is sorted. Usable furniture and goods go to local donation partners; metals and electronics go to recyclers; only what's truly done goes to the transfer station. It's better for the city — and it's often the part clients thank us for most.

One visit, one price

Whether it's three items or a whole floor's worth, you get a fixed price agreed on site before we lift anything, backed by the capacity to finish the job in a single visit. No mid-job dump runs, no "we'll need to come back tomorrow," no second invoice. Serving Toronto, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Mississauga, Vaughan, Brampton, Markham, and Richmond Hill.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you remove junk in Toronto?

Same-day and next-day slots are available across the GTA, subject to crew availability. Book a site valuation online or call — we confirm a 2-hour arrival window, not a vague 'sometime Thursday.'

How is junk removal priced?

On site, before we start. We look at the volume, the weight, the access, and your chosen finish level, then fix a firm price. No phone guesses, no meter running, no surprises at the end.

Can you take items from inside the house, basement, or upstairs?

Yes — that's the point of a full-service crew. We do the carrying, from any floor, with property protection down first. You point; we handle the stairs.

What happens to my stuff after you take it?

It gets sorted: donation partners first for usable goods, certified recyclers for metals and electronics, and the transfer station only for what's left. We're happy to tell you where things went.
