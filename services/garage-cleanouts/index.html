We find your floor again

Every packed garage in the GTA tells the same story: it started with one box that never got unpacked. Then the bikes, the tires, the broken snowblower, the workbench project from 2019 — until one day the car lives in the driveway and the garage belongs to the stuff.

Our crews reverse that story in an afternoon. Everything comes out into daylight. You make keep-or-go calls at speed (we set aside anything that looks personal — photos, documents, that envelope of birthday cards). Donations get staged for local partners. And then the finish begins.

The finish is the point

Any hauler can empty a garage. The transformation is what happens after:

  • The Clear-Out — everything gone, floor broom-cleaned
  • The Standard Reset — cobwebs down from every corner, industrial sweep, shelves wiped
  • The Pristine Transformation — oil stains power-degreased, walls shop-vacuumed, dust blown from every seam, and what stays organized and staged. The like-new finish.

That last one is the photo you'll send to your family group chat. It's also what makes a home list-ready — realtors book it for exactly that reason.

Built for GTA garages

Deep post-war doubles in Etobicoke, narrow laneway garages in Toronto, triple-bay new builds in Vaughan — we've cleared them all. Heavy extractions (workbenches bolted to studs, shelving, old hot tubs beside the garage) are standard work, not surprises.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most single and double garages are done in one visit — typically a few hours depending on volume and finish level. You'll get a firm timeline along with your fixed price at the on-site valuation.

Can you actually remove oil stains from the concrete?

Our Pristine Transformation tier includes power degreasing of oil-stained concrete. Deep, decades-old staining may not vanish entirely, but the difference is dramatic — we'll set honest expectations when we see the floor.

Do I need to be home during the cleanout?

For the sorting stage, yes — keep-or-go calls are yours to make, and it goes fast. For The Clear-Out tier with a pre-agreed plan, many clients hand us the garage code and come home to bare concrete.

What do you do with tools and things that still work?

Anything usable is set aside for your keep pile or staged for donation to local partners — working tools, bikes, and sports gear get a second life instead of a landfill ticket.
