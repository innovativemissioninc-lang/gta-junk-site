Debris removal that keeps your project on schedule

Restoration timelines die in the gap between tear-out and haul-away. A half-sized truck fills up, the crew leaves for the transfer station, and your drying equipment sits idle while soaked drywall blocks the work area. We close that gap: our crews stage on site and load as your team strips, with the capacity to take an entire tear-out in one continuous haul.

What we clear

  • Flood tear-outs — saturated drywall, insulation, carpet, underpad, ruined contents
  • Fire and smoke damage — charred materials and smoke-contaminated contents
  • Full-gut renovation debris — flooring, cabinetry, fixtures, plaster
  • Contents removal ahead of remediation — packed out clean, fast, and documented

Scope note: licensed abatement and remediation (asbestos, mould treatment) stays with your certified remediation contractor — we handle the debris logistics around it.

Paperwork the adjuster actually wants

Insurance work runs on documentation. Every load comes with transfer-station weigh tickets, heavy debris priced transparently by the tonne, itemized invoices, and before/after photos on request — a clean paper trail from tear-out to disposal.

Built for trade accounts

COIs and WSIB confirmation on file before we're on site. Net-30 terms for established accounts. Same-day emergency response across Toronto, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Mississauga, Vaughan, Brampton, Markham, and Richmond Hill — when the call is a burst pipe in February, dispatch answers, not a call centre.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you respond to an emergency tear-out?

Same-day response is available across the GTA for restoration partners, subject to crew scheduling. Emergency calls get priority routing — phone dispatch directly for the fastest window.

What documentation do you provide for insurance claims?

Transfer-station weigh tickets for every load, itemized invoices, tonnage-based pricing for heavy debris, and before/after photos on request — formatted so the file is adjuster-ready.

Do you do the demolition itself?

We handle debris removal and light, non-structural teardown. Structural demolition and licensed abatement stay with your remediation contractor — we keep the site clear so their work never stalls.

Do you offer trade accounts for restoration companies?

Yes — recurring restoration partners get priority dispatch, COIs on file, and Net-30 terms. Set it up once and booking becomes a text message.
