The cleanout nobody wants to organize

An estate cleanout usually lands on one person — an executor, a son or daughter, often from out of town, often on a deadline set by lawyers or a listing agreement. It's physical work tangled up with emotional work. Our job is to carry both.

How we handle it differently

  • Nothing valuable gets buried in a truck. Before loading begins, crews sweep every room, drawer, and box lid for the things that matter: photographs, documents, jewellery, cash, letters. It all goes into a clean, labelled bin that's handed to you — not sorted by us, just protected.
  • Family first, then donation, then disposal. Items family members want are set aside or staged for pickup. Usable furniture and goods go to local donation partners — with receipts for the estate's records where available. Landfill is the last stop, not the first.
  • Discretion as a default. Quiet crews, respectful pace, and no production made of the work. Neighbours see a clean operation, not a spectacle.
  • A finish worthy of the listing. Most estate properties are headed to market. Our Pristine Transformation finish hands your realtor a home that photographs like it's already staged.

Working with your timeline — and your team

We coordinate directly with executors, estate lawyers, realtors, and stagers, and we're comfortable being one moving part in a bigger plan. Need the home cleared before an appraisal Friday? Booked today, transformed tomorrow, photos by the weekend.

Serving families across Toronto, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Mississauga, Vaughan, Brampton, Markham, and Richmond Hill — including the neighbourhoods where these homes hold the most history: the Kingsway, Rosedale, Lawrence Park, and the Beaches.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you handle valuables and personal documents?

Every room is checked before loading. Photos, documents, jewellery, and anything personal goes into a clean, labelled bin handed directly to the executor or family — we protect it, you decide on it.

Can you provide donation receipts for the estate?

Where our donation partners issue receipts, we pass them along for the estate's records, along with an itemized invoice for the cleanout itself.

How quickly can an estate be cleared?

Most homes are cleared in one to two visits. If you're working against a listing or appraisal date, tell us at the valuation — we build the schedule around your deadline.

Can you work directly with our realtor or lawyer?

Yes — we regularly coordinate timing, access, and finish level directly with realtors, stagers, and estate lawyers so out-of-town executors don't have to project-manage from a distance.
