New city, new-house problems

Vaughan's homes are newer and larger than most of the GTA's, and they generate a particular kind of clutter. New-build move-ins leave a wake of packaging, outgrown furniture that didn't survive the upgrade, and builder leftovers stacked exactly where the third car should go. Renovations in Woodbridge and Maple produce debris by the room. And Kleinburg's estate properties have triple garages capable of hiding a small warehouse behind handsome doors.

The move-in reset

Our most-booked Vaughan service is the post-move purge: flattened boxes, outgrown furniture, and the garage pile everyone agreed to deal with later. Sorted donation-first, cleared in one visit, and finished to whichever level you choose. In homes with premium finishes, full property protection is the default rather than an upgrade — that's not a line, it's the reason we don't scuff new drywall.

Renovation debris, worked around your trades

Kitchen and basement renovations are Vaughan's second economy. We coordinate with your contractor's schedule, clear demolition debris in a single continuous haul, and leave a broom-clean site so trades walk into a workspace instead of a wreck. Heavy material is priced transparently with weigh tickets supplied.

Kleinburg's showpiece garages

A triple garage deserves the full treatment: degreased floors, dust-free corners, and whatever stays organised and staged. It's the difference between parking in a garage and parking in a showroom.
