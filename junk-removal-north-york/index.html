Two North Yorks, one standard

Along the ravines — Hoggs Hollow, York Mills, the edges of the Bridle Path — sit some of the largest homes in the city, where a cleanout means multi-room scale, long driveways, and an unspoken expectation of discretion. Up the Yonge corridor through Willowdale, Sheppard and Finch, it's dense condo living and multigenerational households where space runs out faster than family life slows down. Same crew, same protocol, very different logistics.

Estate-scale work at an estate-level pace

For the ravine-lot homes, the full protocol comes out: floor and wall protection through wide halls and finished basements, room-by-room sorting with valuables set aside, and crews who treat a downsizing as careful work rather than a hauling event. The handover is calm, photographable, and ready for whoever comes next.

When the basement stops being a basement

Willowdale's larger households live hard in their homes, and basements, garages and spare rooms absorb the overflow until they're unusable. Clearing the backlog in a single visit — sorted donation-first — is how that space comes back. We also clear ahead of second-suite renovations, then return for the construction debris when your contractor finishes.

The Yonge condo corridor

From Sheppard to Finch, unit turnovers run on building rules. We arrive with certificates of insurance, book the elevator, work inside the window, and leave the unit swept and showable.

What a North York Cleanout Includes

Four commitments that hold on every job, regardless of size or finish level.

Fixed Price

Quoted before anything moves

We walk the space, give you a number in writing, and that number is what you pay. No hourly creep, no surprises when the trailer is full.

Protected

Licensed, insured, careful

Floor runners, door-frame protection, and crews who carry rather than drag. We leave your walls and floors exactly as we found them.
