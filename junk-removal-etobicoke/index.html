The deep garage belongs to Etobicoke

The Kingsway, Sunnylea, Humber Valley and Alderwood were built when a garage was expected to hold a car and a workbench. Seven decades on, they hold everything else: inherited furniture nobody could face selling, three generations of tools, and the lawn equipment arms race that comes with mature trees and a corner lot. These deep singles and doubles are the most dramatic transformation we do — packed to the rafters at nine, bare degreased concrete by dinner.

Long-held homes, handled quietly

The same streets hold Etobicoke's other specialty: the family home owned for forty years. Estate and downsizing clearings in the Kingsway and Princess-Rosethorn get our most discreet crews. Photographs, documents and anything that looks personal go into a clean labelled bin and come to the family — never into the truck. Donations are receipted where our partners issue them, which matters when an estate's paperwork is being reconciled.

Humber Bay's condo shoreline

Along Lake Shore and Park Lawn, the Humber Bay towers turn over constantly. That work runs on building rules rather than muscle: elevator bookings, dock windows, and a COI on file before we arrive. We handle those weekly and hand back a unit that's showable, not just empty.

Why Etobicoke response times are among our fastest

Our crews' routes run the Queensway and the 427, which puts most of Etobicoke inside an easy reach. Same-day slots are frequently available here when they're tight elsewhere in the GTA. If your timing is urgent, it's worth calling rather than assuming.
