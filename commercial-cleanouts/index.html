A commercial clearing is a scheduling problem first

The clearing part of a commercial job is rarely the difficulty. The difficulty is the loading dock booking, the elevator that is only available between six and ten, the certificate of insurance property management needs before anyone is allowed in the building, and the fact that the tenant is still trading until Friday.

We work commercially because we are built for it: insured to the level buildings ask for, registered with the Ministry of the Environment for waste transportation, able to work after hours, and willing to do the administrative part properly — COIs on file, Net-30 invoicing, one statement across a portfolio rather than a transaction per unit.

For property managers in particular, the value is not in any single clearing. It is in having one number to call for every turnover across the portfolio, and knowing the unit will be handed back ready to re-list rather than simply emptied.

Good Questions

Commercial Cleanout — Questions We Get Asked

How much does a commercial cleanout cost in Toronto?

Commercial cleanouts start at $2,500. The number depends on volume, weight, access — ground floor versus a twelfth-floor suite with a booked elevator — and whether the work has to happen after hours. Portfolio and recurring turnover volume is priced separately and more keenly, because repeat work is worth more to us than a one-off. A 30% deposit confirms the booking, waived for established Net-30 accounts.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance before you enter the building?

Yes, and it is normal. We carry $2 million in commercial general liability and will issue a certificate naming the building owner and management company before the first visit. We are also registered with Ontario’s Ministry of the Environment as a waste transportation system, registration R-004-5477911257, which some institutional landlords ask for separately.

Do you work after hours so the business can stay open?

Yes. Evening, overnight and weekend scheduling is standard commercial practice for us, and we work to whatever loading dock and service elevator windows the building allocates. Retail and office tenants rarely need to close.

Do you handle tenant turnovers and leave-behinds for property managers?

Yes — unit turnovers, tenant leave-behinds and multiplex clearing are core commercial work. The unit is handed back swept, fixings removed and ready to show. Where a tenancy is still live, we will ask you to confirm you have the legal right to clear the unit before booking; that protects both parties.

Can we set up an account across multiple properties?

Yes. Portfolio accounts get volume pricing, a direct contact rather than the general queue, certificates of insurance kept on file, purchase order references carried to the invoice, and a single monthly statement covering every site. Ask at the first job and we will set it up.
