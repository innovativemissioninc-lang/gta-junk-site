Three different Auroras

Downtown Aurora holds century homes with the access challenges that come with them — narrow stairs, small doorways, and basements never designed for modern furniture. East of Bayview it's family subdivisions with two-car garages and finished basements. North and west, estate lots with outbuildings. Each needs a different plan and the same standard.

Heritage homes deserve care, not speed

Original trim, plaster walls and hardwood that's been down for a hundred years don't forgive a careless crew. Runners go down the full route, corners get guarded, doorways get padded — before the first item moves, not after the first scuff.

Downsizing after the children leave

Aurora has a high concentration of families now moving from four bedrooms to something simpler. Those clearings run on the family's pace: keep, relatives, donate, go, decided room by room, with photographs and documents protected in a clean bin before anything is loaded.

Renovation debris

Aurora renovates heavily — kitchens, basements, additions. We coordinate around your trades, clear demolition debris in one continuous haul, and leave the site broom-clean so the next contractor walks into a workspace instead of a pile.

Where things actually go

York Region encourages donating usable items rather than disposing of them, which is how we already work — serviceable furniture and tools are staged for local partners before disposal is considered.

Aurora Disposal & Pricing Questions

Where do bulky items and appliances go in York Region?

York Region runs Community Environmental Centres that take bulky items and large appliances — the Vaughan CEC at 130 McCleary Court is the closest to Woodbridge. Disposal fees apply on items dropped as garbage. Verified against York Region's Waste Depots pages, July 2026.

What if it's still in good condition?

York Region encourages donating usable items to charity rather than disposing of them, which matches how we work — anything serviceable is staged for local donation partners before disposal is even considered. Verified against York Region's waste guidance, July 2026.
