Toronto estates are usually harder than the square footage suggests

THE SHORT ANSWEREstate cleanouts in Toronto run $2,000 to $7,000+. In Toronto specifically, access drives the price more than floor area: cellar staircases, attic conversions, laneway garages and permit-only parking make the same volume take materially longer than in a suburban house. Price is fixed in writing at a free on-site assessment, and executors receive dated photographs, weigh tickets and donation receipts for the estate file.

A 1,400 square foot semi in Leslieville can hold more than a 3,000 square foot house in the suburbs, because the storage is vertical and awkward: a cellar you reach by a near-vertical staircase, a third-floor attic conversion, a garage off a laneway that a truck cannot get near. We price Toronto estates on access and carry distance as much as on volume, and we tell you which of the two is driving your number at the assessment.

Laneway access, permit parking and the things that stop a crew

Most Toronto estate jobs have a parking problem before they have a clearing problem. Permit-only streets, laneway garages with a shared right of way, and narrow frontages where a trailer cannot sit legally for a full day. We check the street before we quote and, where the City requires it, we arrange the temporary parking permit rather than discovering the issue on the morning.

Out-of-province executors

A large share of Toronto estates are administered by children who moved away. We work unattended from a lockbox or through the listing agent, send a photographed walkthrough before anything moves, confirm by text what is being set aside, and send a second photographed set at completion. Several of our Toronto executors have never been inside the property.

What the estate file gets

Dated before-and-after photographs, scale tickets for anything priced by weight, donation receipts where the receiving charity issues them, a written list of anything that appeared to have value, and one itemised invoice billed to the estate. Estate lawyers who refer us ask for that package by default.

Good Questions

Estate Cleanouts in Toronto — Questions We Get Asked

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Toronto?

Estate cleanouts in Toronto start at $2,000 for a standard clear and $3,200 for our Signature finish, with complete clearances running to $7,000 and beyond on large or multi-level properties. What moves the number in Toronto specifically is access rather than size — a Leslieville semi with a cellar staircase and laneway parking can cost more than a larger suburban house with a double driveway. The price is fixed in writing at a free on-site assessment and a 30% deposit confirms the booking.

Can you clear a Toronto estate if I live out of province?

Yes, and it is common. We work from a lockbox or through the listing agent, photograph the property before anything moves, confirm by text what is being set aside for the family, and send a second photographed set at completion. Invoicing and payment are handled remotely, billed directly to the estate.

Do you handle the parking permit?

Where the street is permit-only or the frontage will not take a trailer for a full day, yes — we arrange the City of Toronto temporary permit as part of the job. It is one of the more common reasons a Toronto clearing runs over, and it is avoidable.

What happens to photographs, documents and jewellery?

They are recovered and set aside, never loaded. Every room is sorted before it is cleared, and anything that reads as personal goes into labelled bins for the family. On a Signature cleanout you also get a written list of anything that appeared to have value.

How quickly can you clear a property before a listing date?

Most Toronto estate properties are cleared in one to three working days once booked. Tell us the listing or photography date at the assessment and we will book backwards from it so your agent can shoot the morning after we finish.
