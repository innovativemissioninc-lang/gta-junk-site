The oldest housing stock in Durham

Oshawa has more pre-1960 housing than anywhere else in Durham, and that shapes the work. Older homes mean full unfinished basements, detached garages, back sheds, and in the oldest pockets, coal chutes and cold cellars that have been storage since the fifties. When a property like that is finally cleared — usually on an estate — it is a multi-day job, not an afternoon.

Estate clearing in O’Neill and Donevan

The established neighbourhoods north and east of downtown have long-tenure owners and a steady flow of estate work. We handle these the way executors need: every room sorted before anything is loaded, documents and photographs set aside, donation receipts where the charity issues them, dated photographs before and after, and one itemised invoice for the estate accounting.

Rental turnovers and tenant leave-behinds

Oshawa has one of the largest rental markets in Durham, and unit clearing after a tenancy ends is regular work for us. Property managers get certificates of insurance on file, Net-30 terms, weigh tickets and photographed records. Where a tenancy is still live we will ask you to confirm you have the legal right to clear the unit before we book — that protects both of us.

Hoarding work, handled quietly

We get more heavy-clutter calls out of Oshawa than most Durham municipalities, usually from an adult child or a landlord facing an inspection date. Those jobs are done in an unmarked vehicle, with nothing staged on the lawn, at a pace the resident can tolerate. We never photograph hoarding properties for marketing.

Oshawa Cleanout & Disposal Questions

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Oshawa?

Estate cleanouts start at $2,000 for a standard clear and $3,200 for our Signature finish, which includes deep cleaning and a photographed handover. Hoarding projects start at $4,000, pre-listing cleanouts at $1,500, and commercial or tenant-turnover work at $2,500. Fixed in writing at a free on-site valuation; a 30% deposit confirms the booking and comes off the final invoice.

Do you do hoarding cleanouts in Oshawa?

Yes. Tier 1 heavy-clutter properties start at $4,000 and severe properties from $8,000 after a confidential walkthrough. We arrive in an unmarked vehicle, stage nothing on the lawn, hand-sort so cash and documents are recovered, and work at a pace the resident sets. Confirmed biohazard is referred to a licensed remediation partner rather than loaded by us.

How does large item pickup work in Oshawa?

Durham Region collects furniture and large items on your regular biweekly garbage collection day, up to two items per pickup, with a separate truck handling them. Metal goods, electronics and porcelain require appointments. Durham can be reached at 311 within Regional limits, or 905-668-7711. Verified against Durham Region’s Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

Do you work with Oshawa landlords and property managers?

Yes — unit turnovers and tenant leave-behinds are core work. Certificates of insurance on file, Net-30 terms, weigh tickets, dated photographs and one itemised invoice per unit or one monthly statement across a portfolio.

Do you offer same-day service in Oshawa?

Same-day and next-day slots are available subject to crew routing, and restoration and property-management clients get priority dispatch. Call (647) 204-0632.
