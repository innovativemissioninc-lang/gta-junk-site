Toronto's most under-served older neighbourhoods

The former City of York — Weston, Mount Dennis, Silverthorn, Fairbank — holds some of the oldest housing stock in the city, and some of the tightest access. Narrow semi-detached lots, shared driveways, laneway garages and basements reached by stairs built for smaller furniture than we make today.

Why access matters more here than volume

A York basement clearing is rarely about how much there is. It's about how it gets out. Stairs turn sharply, doorways are narrow, and the drive may be shared with a neighbour who needs to get to work. We walk the route before we lift anything, protect the whole path including a shared drive, and carry further rather than force something through a doorframe.

Long-held family homes

York has an unusually high proportion of homes owned by the same family for forty or fifty years. Those clearings are as much emotional as physical, and they turn up more photographs, documents and keepsakes than anywhere else we work. All of it goes into a clean labelled bin and comes to the family before a single box is loaded.

Rentals, rooming houses and turnovers

Much of York's stock is rental, including converted homes and multiplexes. Landlord turnovers here need a crew that clears and sweeps a unit showable in one visit, with COIs supplied and one invoice at the end.

York Disposal & Pricing Questions

Will the City take my old couch and fridge?

Yes. Toronto collects oversized items — appliances, furniture, carpets — on your regular garbage day, set 0.5 metres from your bins and out by 7:00 a.m. Furniture doesn't need dismantling, but doors must come off fridges and ovens. Verified against the City of Toronto Oversized & Metal Items page, July 2026.

What won't they take?

Construction and renovation waste — drywall, wood, and similar — isn't collected at the curb, and neither is hazardous waste like paint or fluorescent tubes. Those need a City Drop-Off Depot. That's the most common reason a renovation clearing stalls here. Verified against the City of Toronto Oversized & Metal Items page, July 2026.

What does a cleanout cost in York?

We price by finish level: The Clear-Out from $495, The Standard Reset from $795, and The Pristine Transformation from $1,195. Your exact price is fixed at a free on-site valuation before a single item moves.
