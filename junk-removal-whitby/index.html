Century homes downtown, and what is in their basements

Downtown Whitby and the streets off Brock have genuine century properties, and century properties have basements that were never meant to be finished and have been used as storage for a hundred years. Low ceilings, narrow stairs, stone foundations and no walk-out. Clearing one is a carry job, not a chute job, and it is priced accordingly — which is exactly the kind of property a volume junk crew underquotes and then renegotiates halfway through.

Brooklin, and the barn-and-outbuilding problem

North of Taunton, Brooklin and the properties running toward the rural edge often come with more than a house: detached shops, barns, outbuildings and yards that have absorbed thirty years of equipment and material. These are among the largest residential jobs in Durham, and they usually need a proper walkthrough before anyone can put a number on them.

Estate and downsizing work

Whitby has a settled, long-tenure population, which in practice means estate clearing and downsizing are most of what we do here. We sort before we load — documents, photographs, jewellery and anything that reads as personal goes into labelled bins for the family rather than into the truck — and we can work unattended through a realtor or lockbox for executors who live elsewhere.

Rolling Acres and the subdivision garages

The 1990s and 2000s subdivisions through Williamsburg and Rolling Acres produce a steady run of garage and basement cleanouts: two cars that have never fit, shelving nobody can reach, a treadmill, and a decade of boxes. Our garage finish takes it back to bare, degreased concrete rather than simply emptying it.

Whitby Cleanout & Disposal Questions

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Whitby?

Estate cleanouts start at $2,000 for a standard clear and $3,200 for our Signature finish. Pre-listing cleanouts start at $1,500, hoarding projects at $4,000, and commercial work at $2,500. The exact number depends on volume, weight, access and finish level, and it is fixed in writing at a free on-site valuation. A 30% deposit confirms the booking and comes off the final invoice.

Do you clear century-home basements in downtown Whitby?

Yes, and we price them honestly. Narrow stairs, low ceilings, stone foundations and no walk-out mean everything comes up by hand, which takes longer and costs more than a modern basement with a side door. We assess that at the valuation rather than discovering it on the day and asking you for more money.

How does large item pickup work in Whitby?

Durham Region collects furniture and large items on your regular biweekly garbage collection day with no appointment needed, up to two items per pickup, with a separate truck handling them. Metal goods, electronics and porcelain require appointments. Durham can be reached at 311 within Regional limits, or 905-668-7711. Verified against Durham Region’s Furniture and Large Item Collection page, July 2026.

Can you clear a Brooklin property with outbuildings?

Yes. Barns, detached shops and yard accumulation are normal work for us, but they need a walkthrough before a price — volume, weight and access vary enormously on rural and semi-rural properties, and anyone quoting one over the phone is guessing in their own favour.
