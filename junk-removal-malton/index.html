The appliance problem, specifically

Because Malton is in Peel, the appliance rule catches people constantly: the Region no longer takes large appliances from the curb. A fridge left at the kerb in Malton simply doesn't get collected. It has to go to a Community Recycling Centre or leave with the retailer. We handle that as part of the load rather than leaving you to arrange a trip.

Good Questions

Malton Disposal & Pricing Questions

Does Peel collect large appliances from the curb here?

No — and it surprises people every time. Peel Region no longer collects large appliances from the curb. Fridges, stoves, washers and dryers have to go to a Peel Community Recycling Centre, where they're accepted free, or leave with your retailer on delivery of the replacement. Verified against Peel Region's Bulky Items page, July 2026.

What counts as a bulky item?

Anything larger than 1 metre (39 inches) in one dimension or heavier than 20 kilograms (44 pounds), furniture included regardless of size. Acceptable bulky items are collected free on your regular garbage day if out by 7:00 a.m. Verified against Peel Region's Bulky Items page, July 2026.
