Garages in Etobicoke

Most Etobicoke garages we see are single or one-and-a-half car, detached at the back of a long driveway in the south end or attached to a side-split further north. They tend to hold decades of tools, paint tins, lawn equipment and the furniture that came out of the basement during a renovation.

We work in The Kingsway, Islington Village, Mimico, New Toronto, Long Branch and Alderwood, and across the City of Toronto. About working in Etobicoke

Etobicoke runs from the Lake Ontario shoreline at Long Branch and Mimico up to Rexdale and the Humber River valley. The south end is older housing stock on deep lots, much of it bought by families in the 1950s and 60s who are now downsizing, which is why basement and garage clear-outs ahead of a sale are so common here. North Etobicoke has more side-splits, townhouses and rental properties that turn over between tenants.

Toronto's curbside bulky-item pickup and drop-off depots work for a few pieces. When the whole space has to go at once, we load, sort and haul it in one visit.

We also cover Markland Wood, Humber Bay Shores, Thistletown and Rexdale. If you are not sure we cover your street, call or text and ask.
