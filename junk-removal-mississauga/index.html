Mississauga Disposal & Pricing Questions

Does Peel collect large appliances from the curb?

No — and this catches people out. Peel Region no longer collects large appliances from the curb. Fridges, stoves, washers and dryers must be taken to a Peel Community Recycling Centre, where they are accepted free of charge, or removed by your retailer on delivery of the replacement. If neither works for you, that is precisely the sort of job we take. Verified against Peel Region's Bulky Items page, July 2026.

What counts as a bulky item in Peel?

Peel defines a bulky item as a household item larger than 1 metre (39 inches) in any one dimension or heavier than 20 kilograms (44 pounds), including furniture of any size or weight. Acceptable bulky items are collected free from the curb on your regular garbage day if set out by 7:00 a.m. Verified against Peel Region's Bulky Items page, July 2026.

Can I take it to a Community Recycling Centre myself?

Yes. Bulky items are accepted at Peel CRCs, though drop-off fees apply for general bulky items (appliances are free). The practical question is whether you have a vehicle, the time, and someone to help you lift — that calculation is usually why people call us. Verified against Peel Region's Bulky Items page, July 2026.
