THE SHORT ANSWERHoarding cleanouts in Toronto start at $4,000 for Tier 1 and run $8,000 to $12,000+ for severe properties. On Toronto semis and row houses the work is planned around discretion: unmarked vehicle, nothing staged on the lawn or porch, continuous loading into a closed truck. Confirmed biohazard is referred to a licensed remediation contractor.

This is the practical difference between a hoarding clearing in Toronto and one in the suburbs. On a semi-detached or row house with a shared wall and a frontage you could step across, there is nowhere to stage anything and no way to work unnoticed unless you plan for it. We arrive in an unmarked vehicle, load continuously from the door to a closed truck, and put nothing on the lawn or the porch. For most of the families who call us, that is the service.

Who actually calls

Almost never the resident. It is an adult child who has just seen inside for the first time in years, a landlord with an inspection date, a case worker, or a sibling arriving from out of town. There is usually a deadline attached and usually a lot of embarrassment. We take the call matter-of-factly and we do not editorialise on site.

Toronto housing stock makes these jobs physical

Cellar stairs you cannot carry down safely, third-floor attic conversions, kitchens that open onto a single narrow hallway. A Toronto hoarding property is rarely one truckload and almost never one day — it is days of room-by-room excavation with the carry distance working against you the whole time. That is why the pricing starts where it does and why we insist on walking it first.

What we will not do

We are registered with Ontario’s Ministry of the Environment for non-hazardous waste. Light surface soiling in an otherwise heavy-clutter property is normal work. Significant animal or human waste, sharps, or conditions that need remediation are outside our registration, and we refer those to a licensed remediation contractor rather than loading them. We tell you at the walkthrough, not halfway through the job.

Toronto Hoarding Cleanout Pricing

Nobody can price a hoarding property honestly over the phone. The walkthrough is free and confidential.

Tier 1 — from $4,000. Heavy clutter, one or two levels, normal structural access. Usually two to four working days.

Good Questions

Hoarding Cleanouts in Toronto — Questions We Get Asked

How much does a hoarding cleanout cost in Toronto?

Tier 1 hoarding cleanouts in Toronto start at $4,000 — heavy clutter, one or two levels, normal access. Severe properties run $8,000 to $12,000 or more and are quoted individually after a confidential walkthrough. In Toronto the carry distance is usually what drives the number: cellar stairs, attic conversions and narrow hallways make the same volume take substantially longer than it would in a suburban house.

Will the neighbours know?

Not from us, and on a Toronto semi or row house that takes planning rather than luck. Unmarked vehicle, no signage, nothing staged on the lawn or porch, continuous loading from the door into a closed truck, and crews briefed to be unremarkable. We also never photograph hoarding jobs for marketing.

Do you handle biohazard, animal waste or needles?

No. Our MECP registration covers non-hazardous waste only. Light surface soiling in an otherwise heavy-clutter property is part of normal work, but significant animal or human waste, sharps, or conditions needing remediation get referred to a licensed remediation contractor. We say so at the walkthrough rather than discovering it mid-job.

My parent does not want this to happen. Can you still help?

Sometimes, and sometimes not yet. We will not force a clearing on someone living in their own home, and a cleanout done against the resident’s will tends to be undone within a year. What works is going slowly — one room, agreed in advance, resident present and holding the final say. Where a therapist, case worker or occupational therapist is involved we take direction from them.

Will you find cash and documents?

Usually, and we look. Hoarded properties very often hold cash, identity documents, deeds, bank books, jewellery and medication distributed through the clutter. We hand-sort rather than shovel, which is slower and is exactly why things get found.

Do you work with Toronto landlords and property managers?

Yes. Unit clearing after an eviction or abandonment is regular work, with certificates of insurance, weigh tickets, dated photographs and an itemised invoice for your file. Where a tenancy is still live we will ask you to confirm you have the legal right to clear the unit before booking.
