Who does whole-property cleanouts in Vaughan?

We do. A property cleanout is a different job from a junk pickup: every room, the basement, the garage, the shed, and whatever accumulated in the crawlspace, cleared in one coordinated project rather than a series of visits. We work across Woodbridge, Maple, Concord and Kleinburg, and we finish the property to the level you choose instead of leaving you with an empty house that still needs cleaning.

What a whole-property cleanout actually involves

The scale is what separates it. A full property means sorting decisions in every room, heavy items down stairs that were never designed for them, and a donation route for the significant share of contents that are still perfectly usable. We walk the property with you first, agree the scope room by room, and fix the price in writing before anything moves. The crew then works the property as one continuous project, so you are not living around an unfinished job for a fortnight.

Estate clearing, handled with discretion

Estate work in Vaughan is frequently a family clearing a parent's home against a deadline set by a lawyer or a realtor. We set aside photographs, documents, jewellery and anything that reads as personal for the family to review before it goes anywhere. Usable furniture and household goods are staged for donation rather than thrown out, which matters to most families more than the disposal cost does. The house is handed back swept and ready for listing photographs.

Rental turnovers and multiplex units

For landlords and property managers, the only measure of a turnover is how quickly the unit can be re-listed. Tenant leave-behinds, abandoned furniture and the contents of a unit that emptied badly are cleared on a scheduled basis, with certificates of insurance on file and trade accounts available. The unit comes back cleared and swept, not merely emptied.

Downsizing and pre-sale purges

Downsizing is the gentlest version of this work and often the slowest, because every decision belongs to the homeowner. We work at the pace the household sets, keep the donate pile visible so nothing disappears without agreement, and clear only what has been agreed. A pre-sale purge has a different goal — the property has to photograph well and show empty — and that is a finish-level decision we will walk you through before we quote it.

Where bulky items and appliances go in York Region

York Region operates Community Environmental Centres and waste depots that accept bulky items and large appliances, including the Vaughan Community Environmental Centre at 130 McCleary Court and the Richmond Hill Community Environmental Centre at 1124 Elgin Mills Road East. Disposal fees apply to items dropped off as garbage, and accepted materials vary by depot — worth confirming before driving across the region with a load. When we handle the property, the depot runs are ours, not yours. Verified against York Region's Waste Depots pages, July 2026.
