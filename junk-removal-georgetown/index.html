Where the curbside system runs out

Halton Hills residents get bulky collection every other week with a strict three-item limit. For a chair and a mattress, that works. For a garage, a basement or an estate, three items a fortnight means a clearing measured in months. Most people discover this two weeks into trying.

Georgetown's older core

The streets around Main Street and Mill Street hold century homes with narrow stairs, small doorways and detached garages down long drives. Access planning matters more than muscle here, and rushing it is how a hundred-year-old doorframe gets damaged.

Newer Georgetown South

The subdivisions bring volume instead: double garages that filled during a move and never recovered, finished basements storing three households' worth of belongings, and renovation debris from finishing them.

Acton and the rural edge

Acton and the surrounding countryside mean outbuildings, workshops and properties where the nearest disposal option is a genuine drive. Metal gets separated for recycling, usable equipment is set aside for donation, and anything with fuel or chemicals in it is routed properly rather than loaded.

Appliances need an appointment

Worth knowing before you plan your weekend: Halton's free metal and appliance collection is by appointment only. An appliance left at the kerb without one simply stays there. Details in the questions below.

Good Questions

Georgetown Disposal & Pricing Questions

Does Halton collect appliances from the curb?

Yes, but only by appointment. Halton offers free curbside metal and appliance collection which you book online or through 311 — it is not an automatic pickup, and an appliance left out without a booking simply stays there. Verified against Halton Region's Metal and Appliance Collection page, July 2026.

How many bulky items will they take?

Bulky waste is collected every other week alongside your garbage, with a hard limit of three items. Additional items are not collected. For a full garage or basement, three items a fortnight is not a workable timeline — that's the gap we fill. Verified against Halton Region's Curbside Collection and Waste Sorting Guide, July 2026.

What does a cleanout cost in Georgetown?

Estate cleanouts start at $2,000, our signature estate finish at $3,200, pre-listing cleanouts at $1,500, hoarding projects at $4,000 and commercial work at $2,500. Your exact price is fixed in writing at a free on-site valuation before a single item moves, and a 30% deposit confirms the booking.

How fast can you get to Georgetown?

Georgetown is roughly twenty-nine kilometres from our base. Same-day and next-day slots are genuinely available here rather than a courtesy line — call (647) 204-0632 for today's window.
